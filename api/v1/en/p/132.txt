Please use "Variable font animation" (可変フォントアニメーション), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #132

What it is: The weight of the letters changes smoothly
What the sample shows: The same word goes smoothly from thin to very bold and back, with a slider below showing the weight.
Spec:
- "MOTION" (112 px, system-ui, 2 px letter spacing). The weight goes back and forth twice per 4.7 s: v = 0.5 − 0.5·cos(2π × 2 × time ÷ 4.7), which eases at both ends.
- Weight = round(100 + 800v), from 100 to 900, set directly as the font weight.
- A slider below (x = 112→427, y = 180) labelled thin on the left and bold on the right. The knob (yellow, radius 14) follows v, with the current weight shown above it.
When to use it: Use it on a title, a logo or a single word to show loudness or emotion through the letters themselves.
