Please use "Page curl" (ページめくり), one of the names in "Motion graphics moves, part 2".
Motion Prompt #140

What it is: Peels back like paper to show the next page
What the sample shows: A page peels back from its corner and folds over, revealing the next page beneath.
Spec:
- Page 1 (white) → page 2 (cream) → page 1, 2.35 s each. The curl starts at the bottom-right corner, 0.35–1.75 s (1.4 s), quadratic ease-in-out.
- The fold line moves from the corner in the direction (−1, −0.42), normalised, by 0→600 px.
- The part beyond the fold line is mirrored across it to form the flap, the back of the page (light grey or beige), with a drop shadow (blur 14, offset 4, 4) and a white 2 px edge.
- The next page shows where the paper has lifted, with a 50 px shadow gradient along the fold.
When to use it: Use it for books, catalogues, albums or chapter breaks, where a paper feel or reading on suits the content.
